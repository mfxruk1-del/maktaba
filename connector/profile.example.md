HOW THE OWNER WANTS THE RESEARCH DONE (copy this file to profile.md and write your own rules; the connector adds them to its
instructions, so every chat that uses the connector follows them. Keep it short: it is read at the start of every chat.)
- AUTHORITY BY FIELD. Which scholars you lean on in each field (creed, fiqh, hadith, language, ...), and that a scholar or work
  named in the question takes precedence.
- LABELS. Which authors are shown with a label (see labels.json), and how to speak about them.
- DEPTH. When a short answer is enough and when to search deeply.
- FORM. What the answers are for (study, practice, discussion), the language of the answers, and whether Arabic is translated.
- CHECKING. Who checks the findings, and what they need on the page (book, volume, page, the Arabic).
